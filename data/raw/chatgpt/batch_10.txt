 === ANSWER 2 ===

 **Permutations** count arrangements where **order matters**.

 **Combinations** count selections where **order does not matter**.

 For example, suppose you have three people: A, B, and C, and you need to choose two.

 - **Permutations:** AB and BA are different, because the order matters. There are $3\times2=6$ arrangements.
- **Combinations:** AB and BA are the same selection, because order doesn't matter. There are $\binom{3}{2}=3$ selections: AB, AC, BC.

  Learn more  In short: **permutation = arrangement; combination = selection.**

 === ANSWER 3 ===

 Vaccines train the immune system by exposing it to a **safe form or piece of a pathogen**, such as a protein or an inactivated pathogen, without causing the disease the pathogen normally causes.

 The immune system responds by:

 1. **Recognizing the antigen** as foreign.
2. **Activating immune cells**, including B cells and T cells.
3. **Producing antibodies** and other immune defenses.
4. **Creating memory cells** that remain after the initial response.

 Later, if the person encounters the actual pathogen, these memory cells can trigger a **faster and stronger immune response**, helping prevent infection or reduce the severity of illness.

 === ANSWER 4 ===

 **Weather** describes the short-term state of the atmosphere—what is happening over minutes, hours, or days. Examples include today's temperature, rainfall, wind, or thunderstorms.

 **Climate** describes the long-term patterns and averages of weather in a region, typically considered over decades or longer. Examples include a region's typical summer temperatures or long-term rainfall patterns.

 A simple way to remember it:

 - **Weather:** what is happening now or soon.
- **Climate:** what is typical over a long period.

 === ANSWER 5 ===

 The area under $y=x^2$ from $x=0$ to $x=3$ is the definite integral:

 $$
\int_0^3 x^2\,dx
$$

 The antiderivative of $x^2$ is $\frac{x^3}{3}$, so:

 $$
\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^3
=\frac{3^3}{3}-\frac{0^3}{3}
=\frac{27}{3}
=\boxed{9}
$$

 So the area is **9 square units**.

 A **democracy** is a system in which political authority ultimately comes from the people, typically through voting and other forms of political participation.

 A **republic** is a form of government in which the country is considered a public political entity and government authority is exercised through representatives and established laws or institutions.

 The terms can overlap: **a country can be both democratic and a republic**. For example, citizens may elect representatives who make laws on their behalf.

 In simple terms:

 - **Democracy:** emphasizes **popular participation and political authority of the people**.
- **Republic:** emphasizes **representative government and rule through established institutions/laws**.
