=== ANSWER 1 ===

 The **water cycle** is the continuous movement of water between Earth’s surface, the atmosphere, and underground.

 The main stages are:

 - **Evaporation:** Heat from the Sun turns liquid water from oceans, lakes, and rivers into water vapor.
- **Condensation:** Water vapor cools and forms tiny droplets, creating clouds.
- **Precipitation:** Water falls from clouds as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
- **Collection:** Water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers, glaciers, and underground reservoirs.
- **Transpiration:** Plants release water vapor into the atmosphere through their leaves.

 These processes continually recycle Earth’s water.

 === ANSWER 2 ===

 **Proof by contradiction:**

 Suppose that $\sqrt{2}$ is rational. Then it can be written as a fraction in lowest terms:

 $$
\sqrt{2}=\frac{a}{b}
$$

 where $a$ and $b$ are integers, $b\neq0$, and $a,b$ have no common factor.

 Squaring both sides:

 $$
2=\frac{a^2}{b^2}
$$

 so

 $$
a^2=2b^2.
$$

 Thus $a^2$ is even, which means $a$ must be even. Write $a=2k$.

 Substituting:

 $$
(2k)^2=2b^2
$$

 $$
4k^2=2b^2
$$

 $$
b^2=2k^2.
$$

 Therefore $b^2$ is even, so $b$ is also even.

 But then both $a$ and $b$ are even, contradicting our assumption that $\frac ab$ was in lowest terms.

 Therefore,

 $$
\boxed{\sqrt{2}\text{ is irrational}.}
$$

 === ANSWER 4 ===

 A **virus** and a **bacterium** are both microscopic infectious agents, but they are fundamentally different.

 | Feature | Virus | Bacterium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structure | Genetic material surrounded by a protein coat | Complete single-celled organism |
| Living organism? | Generally not considered independently living | Yes, a living cell |
| Size | Usually much smaller | Usually larger |
| Reproduction | Must infect a host cell to make more viruses | Can reproduce independently by cell division |
| Treatment | Antibiotics do not work against viruses | Some bacterial infections can be treated with antibiotics |
| Examples | Influenza virus, HIV, SARS-CoV-2 | _E. coli_, _Streptococcus_ |

In simple terms, **bacteria are living cells**, whereas **viruses are genetic material packaged in a structure that must use a host cell to reproduce**.

 === ANSWER 5 ===

 We want to evaluate

 $$
\int 2x e^{x^2}\,dx.
$$

 Use the substitution

 $$
u=x^2.
$$

 Then

 $$
du=2x\,dx.
$$

 So the integral becomes

 $$
\int e^u\,du=e^u+C.
$$

 Substituting back $u=x^2$:

 $$
\boxed{e^{x^2}+C}.
$$
