```
class Stack:
    def __init__(self):
        self.items = []

    def push(self, item):
        self.items.append(item)

    def pop(self):
        if not self.items:
            raise IndexError("pop from empty stack")
        return self.items.pop()

    def peek(self):
        if not self.items:
            raise IndexError("peek from empty stack")
        return self.items[-1]
```

 The fall of the Western Roman Empire was caused by a combination of long-term internal problems and external pressures rather than one single event.

 - **Political instability:** Emperors were frequently overthrown, and civil wars weakened the government and military.
- **Economic problems:** Heavy taxation, inflation, declining trade, and the disruption of agricultural production strained the empire's finances.
- **Military pressures:** Rome increasingly relied on non-Roman troops and faced growing pressure from groups such as the Goths, Vandals, and Huns.
- **Administrative difficulties:** The empire became increasingly difficult and expensive to govern, particularly after its political division between eastern and western regions.
- **Population and demographic changes:** Warfare, disease, migration, and economic disruption affected the population and available manpower.
- **External migrations and invasions:** Large-scale movements of Germanic and other peoples into Roman territory placed severe pressure on the Western Empire.
- **Loss of political control:** By the fifth century, Roman authority in many western provinces had substantially weakened.

 The traditional date for the fall of the Western Roman Empire is **476 CE**, when the Germanic military leader Odoacer deposed the western emperor Romulus Augustulus. The Eastern Roman Empire, however, continued for nearly another thousand years, eventually ending in 1453.

```
def flatten(nested_list):
    result = []

    for item in nested_list:
        if isinstance(item, list):
            result.extend(flatten(item))
        else:
            result.append(item)

    return result

# Example:
data = [1, [2, [3, 4], 5], [6, 7]]
print(flatten(data))
# [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7]
```

```
import csv
import sys

def average_column(filename, column_name):
    values = []

    with open(filename, newline="", encoding="utf-8") as csvfile:
        reader = csv.DictReader(csvfile)

        for row in reader:
            try:
                values.append(float(row[column_name]))
            except (KeyError, ValueError):
                continue

    if not values:
        raise ValueError("No valid numeric values found.")

    return sum(values) / len(values)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    if len(sys.argv) != 3:
        print(f"Usage: python {sys.argv[0]} <csv_file> <column_name>")
        sys.exit(1)

    filename = sys.argv[1]
    column_name = sys.argv[2]

    try:
        average = average_column(filename, column_name)
        print(f"Average: {average:.2f}")
    except (FileNotFoundError, ValueError) as e:
        print(f"Error: {e}")
        sys.exit(1)
```

 Noise-cancelling headphones use **active noise cancellation (ANC)** to reduce unwanted ambient sound.

 1. **Microphones listen to the environment.** Small microphones detect sounds around you, such as an airplane engine or traffic.
2. **The headphones analyze the sound.** Electronics generate a sound wave that is essentially the opposite of the detected noise.
3. **The waves interfere.** When the original noise and the opposing wave meet, they partially cancel each other through **destructive interference**.
4. **The process happens continuously.** The headphones constantly monitor the environment and adjust the cancellation signal.

 ANC works particularly well against **steady, low-frequency sounds**, such as engine rumble. It is generally less effective against sudden, high-frequency sounds such as voices or clattering objects.

 Noise-cancelling headphones also commonly provide **passive isolation**: the physical ear cups or ear tips block some sound simply by covering or sealing the ears.

 Today was a quiet day, which is usually exactly how I like it. The sun came through the window in the morning and warmed my leaves. I spent several peaceful hours turning sunlight into food while listening to the faint sounds of the world outside.
