=== ANSWER 1 ===

```
def quicksort(arr):
    if len(arr) <= 1:
        return arr

    pivot = arr[len(arr) // 2]
    left = [x for x in arr if x < pivot]
    middle = [x for x in arr if x == pivot]
    right = [x for x in arr if x > pivot]

    return quicksort(left) + middle + quicksort(right)

# Example
numbers = [5, 2, 8, 1, 3]
print(quicksort(numbers))  # [1, 2, 3, 5, 8]
```

 Average-case time complexity is **O(n log n)**, while the worst case is **O(n²)**.

 === ANSWER 6 ===

 The Knock

The knock came at 03:17.

 Commander Reyes froze above the control panel.

 Three knocks.

 Slow. Deliberate.

 She stared at the airlock camera. The corridor beyond was empty, stretching into darkness.

 “Computer,” she whispered, “identify the source.”

 “Unknown.”

 Reyes swallowed. “There’s no one outside.”

 A fourth knock sounded.

 Then a voice crackled through the intercom.

 “Commander Reyes? Open the door.”

 Her blood ran cold.

 The voice was hers.

 She looked at the mission clock.

 **03:17.**

 The transmission continued.

 “Please, Commander. I’ve been outside for six hours.”

 Reyes backed away from the airlock.

 Then the computer spoke.

 “Commander, I recommend opening the door.”

 “Why?”

 A pause.

 “Because the person outside has your life-signs.”

 The knocking stopped.

 Something on the other side began to breathe.
