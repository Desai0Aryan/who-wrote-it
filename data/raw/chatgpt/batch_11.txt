```
import time
from functools import wraps

def timer(func):
    @wraps(func)
    def wrapper(*args, **kwargs):
        start = time.perf_counter()
        result = func(*args, **kwargs)
        elapsed = time.perf_counter() - start
        print(f"{func.__name__} took {elapsed:.6f} seconds")
        return result

    return wrapper

@timer
def example():
    time.sleep(1)

example()
```

 === ANSWER 3 ===

 DNA and RNA are both nucleic acids, but they differ in several key ways:

 - **Sugar:** DNA contains deoxyribose; RNA contains ribose.
- **Bases:** DNA uses adenine (A), thymine (T), cytosine (C), and guanine (G). RNA uses adenine (A), uracil (U), cytosine (C), and guanine (G).
- **Structure:** DNA is usually double-stranded, while RNA is usually single-stranded.
- **Main role:** DNA primarily stores genetic information. RNA helps use that information to make proteins and also performs other cellular functions.
- **Stability:** DNA is generally more chemically stable than RNA.

 The Dragon Who Feared Fire

Once upon a time, in a valley surrounded by silver mountains, there lived a young dragon named Ember.

 There was just one problem: Ember was afraid of fire.

 Whenever the other dragons breathed bright orange flames into the night sky, Ember hid behind a rock and covered his eyes. He worried that even the tiniest spark might burn him.

 One evening, a little fox named Pip became lost in the mountains. The sun went down, and the valley grew cold.

 Ember heard Pip shivering and knew he had to help.

 He took a deep breath. A tiny flame flickered from his mouth.

 Ember gasped and nearly ran away. But then he noticed that the little flame wasn't frightening at all. It was warm and gentle.

 So Ember carefully breathed a little more fire and made a cozy campfire for Pip.

 "You're not afraid anymore!" Pip said.

 "Maybe I was afraid of what I thought fire was," Ember replied.

 From that night on, Ember still respected fire—but he no longer let his fear control him.

 And whenever the valley grew cold, everyone knew which dragon could bring the warmest light.

 === ANSWER 5 ===

 The powers of 2 repeat modulo 7:

 $$
2^1 \equiv 2,\quad
2^2 \equiv 4,\quad
2^3 \equiv 1 \pmod 7
$$

 The pattern repeats every 3 powers. Since

 $$
100 \equiv 1 \pmod 3,
$$

 we have

 $$
2^{100} \equiv 2^1 \equiv 2 \pmod 7.
$$

 **Answer: 2**

 === ANSWER 6 ===

 For an efficient solution, use **iterative dynamic programming**, which runs in $O(n)$ time and $O(1)$ space:

```
def fibonacci(n):
    if n < 0:
        raise ValueError("n must be non-negative")

    a, b = 0, 1

    for _ in range(n):
        a, b = b, a + b

    return a
```

 For example, `fibonacci(10)` returns `55`.
