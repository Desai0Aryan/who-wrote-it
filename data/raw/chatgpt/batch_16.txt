 === ANSWER 2 ===

 There are 8 marbles total, with 3 red.

 The probability of drawing two red marbles without replacement is:

 $$
\frac{3}{8}\times\frac{2}{7}=\frac{6}{56}=\boxed{\frac{3}{28}}
$$

 So the probability is **3/28**, or about **10.7%**.

 Lisbon Travel Blog Intro

Lisbon has a way of making a weekend feel much longer than it really is. From the moment you arrive, the Portuguese capital draws you in with its sunlit hills, tiled façades, winding cobblestone streets, and the unmistakable yellow trams rattling through historic neighborhoods.  === ANSWER 6 ===

 For

 $$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},
$$

 find the eigenvalues from the characteristic equation:

 $$
\det(A-\lambda I)
=
\begin{vmatrix}
2-\lambda&1\\
1&2-\lambda
\end{vmatrix}
=0.
$$

 Thus,

 $$
(2-\lambda)^2-1=0
$$

 $$
(2-\lambda-1)(2-\lambda+1)=0
$$

 so the eigenvalues are

 $$
\boxed{\lambda_1=3,\quad \lambda_2=1}.
$$
