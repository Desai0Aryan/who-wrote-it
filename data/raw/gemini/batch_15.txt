=== ANSWER 1 ===
To solve the quadratic equation $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$, we can factor the quadratic expression by finding two numbers that multiply to $6$ and add up to $-5$. These numbers are $-2$ and $-3$.

$$(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0$$

Setting each factor equal to zero gives:

* $x - 2 = 0 \implies x = 2$
* $x - 3 = 0 \implies x = 3$

Thus, the solutions are **$x = 2$** and **$x = 3$**.

Here is a Python function that reads a text file, normalizes the text (converts to lowercase and removes punctuation), and counts the frequency of each word using a `collections.Counter`:

```python
from collections import Counter
import string


def count_word_frequencies(filepath):
  """Counts the frequency of each word in a given text file."""
  try:
    with open(filepath, "r", encoding="utf-8") as file:
      text = file.read()

    # Normalize text: convert to lowercase and remove punctuation
    translator = str.maketrans("", "", string.punctuation)
    cleaned_text = text.translate(translator).lower()

    # Split into individual words
    words = cleaned_text.split()

    # Count frequencies
    word_counts = Counter(words)
    return word_counts

  except FileNotFoundError:
    print(f"Error: The file '{filepath}' was not found.")
    return Counter()

```

=== ANSWER 4 ===
Ocean tides are primarily caused by the gravitational pull exerted by the Moon and, to a lesser extent, the Sun, combined with Earth's rotation.

* **Gravitational Force:** The Moon's gravity pulls on Earth's oceans. Because the side of Earth closest to the Moon experiences a stronger gravitational pull than the center of Earth, the water bulges outward toward the Moon, creating a high tide.
* **Inertia / Centrifugal Effect:** On the exact opposite side of Earth, the gravitational pull is weakest, and Earth's inertial forces create a second water bulge away from the Moon, creating another high tide.
* **Low Tides:** As Earth rotates through these water bulges, different coastal regions cycle between high and low tides (typically experiencing two high tides and two low tides every 24 hours and 50 minutes).
* **Spring and Neap Tides:** When the Sun, Earth, and Moon align (during full and new moons), their combined gravitational forces produce higher-than-average high tides and lower-than-average low tides called **spring tides**. When they are at a right angle, their forces partially cancel each other out, resulting in milder tides called **neap tides**.

Mitochondria are membrane-bound organelles commonly known as the **"powerhouses of the cell."** Their primary function is to generate the chemical energy needed to power the cell's biochemical reactions through a process called **cellular respiration**, which converts nutrients and oxygen into adenosine triphosphate (ATP). Beyond energy production, mitochondria regulate cellular metabolism, calcium signaling, cellular differentiation, and programmed cell death (apoptosis).
