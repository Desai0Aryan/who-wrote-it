Ada Lovelace (1815–1852) was an English mathematician and writer, chiefly known for her work on Charles Babbage's proposed mechanical general-purpose computer, the Analytical Engine.

She is widely regarded as the **world's first computer programmer**. Her importance stems from:

* **First Algorithm:** She recognized that the machine had applications beyond pure calculation and published the first algorithm intended to be carried out by such a machine (specifically, calculating Bernoulli numbers).
* **Visionary Insight:** She theorized that computers could manipulate any symbols, anticipating the modern era of computing well beyond mere number-crunching.

The fundamental difference lies in how rules and outputs are generated:

* **Traditional Programming:** Humans write explicit rules (code) and provide data as input. The computer processes the data using these rules to produce an output.
* **Machine Learning:** Humans provide input data and the corresponding outputs (answers). The computer uses algorithms to discover the underlying patterns and rules on its own.

=== ANSWER 5 ===
To simplify the expression $(x^2 - 9) / (x - 3)$:

1. **Factor the numerator:** The numerator $x^2 - 9$ is a difference of squares, which factors into $(x - 3)(x + 3)$.
2. **Rewrite the expression:**

$$\frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{x - 3}$$


3. **Cancel the common factor:** For $x \neq 3$, the $(x - 3)$ terms cancel out.
4. **Result:**
$x + 3$

=== ANSWER 6 ===
To compute $17 \times 23$ without a calculator, you can use the distributive property:

1. **Break down one of the numbers:**
$23 = 20 + 3$
2. **Set up the multiplication:**
$17 \times (20 + 3)$
3. **Multiply separately:**
* $17 \times 20 = 340$
* $17 \times 3 = 51$


4. **Add the results together:**
$340 + 51 = 391$

Result: **391**
