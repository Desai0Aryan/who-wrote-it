=== ANSWER 1 ===
The water cycle (also known as the hydrologic cycle) describes the continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth.

* **Evaporation:** The sun heats up water in oceans, lakes, and rivers, turning it into water vapor that rises into the air. Plants also release water vapor through **transpiration**.
* **Condensation:** As the water vapor rises, it cools and turns back into liquid water droplets, forming clouds and fog.
* **Precipitation:** When the water droplets in clouds become too heavy, they fall back to the Earth as rain, snow, sleet, or hail.
* **Collection (Runoff):** Water flows over the land and collects in oceans, rivers, and lakes, or seeps into the ground as groundwater, starting the cycle all over again.

To prove that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational, we use a proof by contradiction.

1. **Assume the contrary:** Assume that $\sqrt{2}$ is a rational number. This means it can be expressed as a fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ in simplest form, where $a$ and $b$ are integers with no common factors other than 1, and $b \neq 0$.
2. **Square both sides:**

$$\sqrt{2} = \frac{a}{b} \implies 2 = \frac{a^2}{b^2} \implies a^2 = 2b^2$$


3. **Analyze $a$:** Since $a^2$ is equal to $2$ times an integer ($b^2$), $a^2$ must be an even number. This implies that $a$ itself must also be an even number. Therefore, we can write $a = 2k$ for some integer $k$.
4. **Substitute $a$ back in:**

$$(2k)^2 = 2b^2 \implies 4k^2 = 2b^2 \implies b^2 = 2k^2$$


5. **Analyze $b$:** This equation shows that $b^2$ is also even, which means $b$ must be even as well.
6. **Conclusion:** If both $a$ and $b$ are even, they share a common factor of $2$. This contradicts our initial assumption that $\frac{a}{b}$ is in simplest form. Therefore, our original assumption must be false, meaning **$\sqrt{2}$ is irrational**.

=== ANSWER 4 ===
While both can cause illness, bacteria and viruses are fundamentally different:

* **Nature of Life:** Bacteria are living, single-celled organisms that have their own machinery to grow, metabolize, and reproduce independently. Viruses are not considered living organisms; they are essentially genetic material (DNA or RNA) enclosed in a protein coat that cannot reproduce without a host cell.
* **Size:** Bacteria are much larger (typically measured in micrometers), whereas viruses are microscopic and significantly smaller (measured in nanometers).
* **Treatment:** Bacterial infections are generally treated with **antibiotics**, which kill the bacteria or stop them from multiplying. Viruses do not respond to antibiotics; instead, viral infections are managed with supportive care, antiviral medications, or prevented using vaccines.

=== ANSWER 5 ===
To evaluate the indefinite integral $\int 2x e^{x^2} \, dx$, we can use the method of substitution (u-substitution).

1. **Choose a substitution:** Let $u = x^2$.
2. **Find the derivative:** The differential $du$ is:

$$du = 2x \, dx$$


3. **Substitute into the integral:** Notice that $2x \, dx$ is already present in the original integral. Substituting $u$ and $du$ gives:

$$\int e^u \, du$$


4. **Integrate:** The integral of $e^u$ with respect to $u$ is simply $e^u + C$:

$$e^u + C$$


5. **Substitute back:** Replace $u$ with $x^2$:

$$e^{x^2} + C$$



**Answer:** $\int 2x e^{x^2} \, dx = e^{x^2} + C$
