# Prompt 2: Rectangle perimeter 36, length = 2 * width. Find area.
# P = 2 * (L + W) = 36 => L + W = 18. L = 2W => 3W = 18 => W = 6, L = 12. Area = L * W = 72.
import sympy as sp
W = sp.Symbol('W')
eq = sp.Eq(2 * (2*W + W), 36)
w_val = sp.solve(eq, W)[0]
l_val = 2 * w_val
area = l_val * w_val
print(f"Prompt 2: W={w_val}, L={l_val}, Area={area}")

=== ANSWER 1 ===
The sky is blue due to a phenomenon called **Rayleigh scattering**. Sunlight reaches Earth's atmosphere and is scattered in all directions by all the gases and particles in the air. Because light travels in waves, different colors of light have different wavelengths. Blue light travels in smaller, shorter waves than other colors, meaning it gets scattered more than the other colors, causing us to see a blue sky when we look up.

=== ANSWER 2 ===
To find the area of the rectangle, we can set up an algebraic equation based on the given information:

* Let the width be $W$ and the length be $L$.
* We are given that the length is twice the width: $L = 2W$.
* The perimeter of a rectangle is given by the formula $P = 2(L + W) = 36$.

Substitute $L = 2W$ into the perimeter formula:
$2(2W + W) = 36$
$2(3W) = 36$
$6W = 36$
$W = 6$

Now, find the length:
$L = 2(6) = 12$

Finally, calculate the area:
$\text{Area} = L \times W = 12 \times 6 = 72$.

The area of the rectangle is **72**.

To solve the system of equations:

1. $2x + y = 7$
2. $x - y = 2$

We can use the elimination method by adding the two equations together to eliminate $y$:
$(2x + y) + (x - y) = 7 + 2$
$3x = 9$
$x = 3$

Now, substitute $x = 3$ back into the second equation to find $y$:
$3 - y = 2$
$-y = -1$
$y = 1$

The solution to the system is **$x = 3$ and $y = 1$** (or the ordered pair $(3, 1)$).

To find the original price of the shirt:

* A 20% discount means you pay $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$ of the original price.
* Let $P$ be the original price.
* $0.80 \times P = 40$

To solve for $P$:
$P = \frac{40}{0.80} = 50$

The original price of the shirt was **$50**.
