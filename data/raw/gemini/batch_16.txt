To find the probability of drawing two red marbles without replacement, we calculate the probability of each independent draw:

* The bag contains a total of $3 + 5 = 8$ marbles, with $3$ of them being red. The probability of drawing a red marble on the first try is $\frac{3}{8}$.
* After removing one red marble, there are now $7$ marbles left in the bag, including $2$ red marbles. The probability of drawing a second red marble is $\frac{2}{7}$.

Multiplying these probabilities together:


$$\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{2}{7} = \frac{6}{56} = \frac{3}{28}$$

The probability that both drawn marbles are red is $\frac{3}{28}$ (approximately **10.7%**).

To solve for $x$ in the equation $3x + 7 = 25$:

1. Subtract $7$ from both sides:

$$3x = 25 - 7$$


$$3x = 18$$


2. Divide both sides by $3$:

$$x = \frac{18}{3}$$


$$x = 6$$

To find the eigenvalues of the matrix


$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix},$$


we solve the characteristic equation $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$, where $I$ is the identity matrix and $\lambda$ represents the eigenvalues.

$$\det \begin{pmatrix} 2 - \lambda & 1 \\ 1 & 2 - \lambda \end{pmatrix} = 0$$

Calculate the determinant:


$$(2 - \lambda)(2 - \lambda) - (1)(1) = 0$$

$$(2 - \lambda)^2 - 1 = 0$$

Factor or solve for $\lambda$:


$$(2 - \lambda)^2 = 1$$

$$2 - \lambda = \pm 1$$

* If $2 - \lambda = 1$, then $\lambda = 1$.
* If $2 - \lambda = -1$, then $\lambda = 3$.

The eigenvalues of the matrix are **$1$** and **$3$**.
